I am an undergraduate in mathematics and geophysics at Stanford. I work in number theory, combinatorics, and probability, and at the interface of computation and mathematical research.

I am a founding engineer at the Large Math Initiative. Before Stanford I was a student in MIT PRIMES-USA and at the MIT Research Science Institute. I grew up in Portland, Oregon. I teach mathematics — most recently through Newton Academy and AwesomeMath — and I spend what time I can in the mountains.

Current work

At the Large Math Initiative I build the corpus that feeds a platform for attacking open problems with frontier models; a first result is a note on a conjecture of Altschuler and Chewi on shifted Rényi divergence. I also lead the mathematical modeling on a hail-forecasting project for Munich Re.
