I am an undergraduate in mathematics at Stanford. I work mainly in number theory, with adjacent interests in combinatorics, probability, and the use of computation in pure mathematics.

Before coming to Stanford I was a student in MIT PRIMES-USA and at the MIT Research Science Institute. I founded The Math Aces, a nonprofit that teaches mathematics to children.

Current work

I recently wrote a short note on a conjecture of Altschuler and Chewi concerning shifted Rényi divergence: the claimed closed form holds for Kullback–Leibler divergence and fails for every order q > 1. I am also finishing a manuscript from RSI on weak error bounds for stochastic linear multistep methods, and continuing computational work on extremal unit-distance graphs.
